📖 What is Çukur?

New here? Çukur is a Turkish crime drama that aired on Show TV from 2017 to 2021 — 4 seasons, 131 episodes. It tells the story of the Koçovalı family and their control over Çukur ("The Pit"), a dangerous neighborhood in Istanbul's historic Balat district.

🎬

The Show

A crime drama set in Istanbul, Turkey. Show TV, 2017–2021. Winner of multiple Turkish TV awards.

👨‍👩‍👦

The Family

The Koçovalıs — İdris (father), and sons Cumali, Kahraman, Selim and Yamaç — rule the neighborhood with a strict code of honor.

🚫

The Rule

Drugs are strictly forbidden in Çukur. Anyone who breaks this ban faces the family's wrath. That's the law of the Mahalle.

🩸

The Story

When the family's control is threatened, youngest son Yamaç — a chemist who wanted nothing to do with the business — returns home and is forced to become the leader.
